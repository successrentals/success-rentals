HOW IT WORKS

Simple, Transparent & Professional Process

From the moment you contact us until your property is successfully rented, Success Rentals manages every step with complete transparency and professionalism.

01

Contact Success Rentals

Contact us through Phone or WhatsApp and share your property details with our team.

02

Property Discussion

We understand your property, rental expectations, preferred tenants and recommend the most suitable service plan.

03

Agreement & Plan Selection

A professional agreement is executed wherever applicable before key handover and services begin.

04

Tenant Search & Visits

Tenant sourcing, shortlisting, visit scheduling, property showing and feedback are coordinated according to the selected plan.

05

Tenant Finalization

The owner meets the shortlisted tenant and rental terms are finalized. Applicable charges are collected based on the selected plan.

06

Property Successfully Rented

Congratulations! Your property is rented. Rental Coordination Charges stop from the month in which the property is successfully rented.
